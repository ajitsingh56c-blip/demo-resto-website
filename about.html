About Ajit Restaurant

A culinary journey rooted in authentic heritage, soulful recipes, and the timeless joy of dining together.

20+ Years Mastery & Tradition
Our Philosophy

Taste That Brings Everyone Together

"When authentic spices meet patient, slow-cooking techniques and heartfelt hospitality, food ceases to be mere meals—it turns into lifelong cherished memories."

Founded with an enduring mission to serve authentic royal Indian flavors in a contemporary, welcoming ambiance, Ajit Restaurant has grown to become a beloved culinary landmark in Delhi NCR.

From our legendary Butter Chicken simmered with fenugreek butter to our Dum Biryani cooked in sealed earthenware handis, every plate is crafted with utmost devotion and pride.
