Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining, ordering, and party bookings at Ajit Restaurant.

Do you serve Pure Vegetarian and Jain food options?

Yes! We have a dedicated Pure Vegetarian kitchen section with separate preparation vessels. We prepare custom Jain dishes (without onion, garlic, or root vegetables) on request.

How do I place an online food delivery or pickup order?

Navigate to our Food Menu page, click "Add" on any item, open the cart drawer, and complete your order. We deliver within 30–45 minutes!

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

Is valet parking available at Ajit Restaurant?

Yes, complimentary valet parking is offered at our main portico for all lunch and dinner dining guests.
